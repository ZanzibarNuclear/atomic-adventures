# Story Overview

[DRAFT] — Beginning storyline and narrative direction for Atomic Adventures.

## Overview

The game opens in the woods — not at the facility. **Zanzibar Nuhero** walks alone through a pine forest on the side of a mountain, hungry, his canteen nearly empty, still pressing onward with undying optimism. He is thinking about the argument that split his group apart to look for help. We do not yet know who they are, what the argument was about, or what kind of help they need.

That walk leads somewhere: a vast, dormant energy complex — a central facility that once tied together hydro, nuclear, solar, and fusion installations. The machines are intact. The knowledge to run them is gone. Something happened here, and Zanzibar intends to find out.

The player follows Zanzibar as he restores each installation, facility by facility. Every success brings light, sound, and motion back to a quiet world — and reveals another piece of a larger mystery: _Where did everyone go? Why was this knowledge lost? And what becomes possible when the power comes back on?_

**Story structure:** The game is divided into parts. **Part I** spans one week at the surface campus — from the pine forest through first power and beyond — and ends when Zanzibar discovers a **hidden elevator that goes down**. _The End… and the beginning of Part II._

Character profile and art references: [Characters](characters.md).

### Part I — One week at the facility

Part I plays out over **roughly one week**. The player experiences time passing — day/night cycles, meals, rest — but **days are a pacing container, not a rigid schedule**. Beats do not map one-to-one to calendar days. Even the early storyline already described (arrival, shelter, library, hydro, buggy) may unfold across **several days**, longer than a quick read might suggest.

**Design intent:** The week should feel lived-in. Zanzibar has time to explore, get stuck, learn, sleep on a problem, and try again. Rushing from forest to power in two days would undermine both the mystery and the learning.

## Opening — The Pine Forest

**When:** Story start. Before the player sees any facility.

**Where:** A pine forest on a mountainside. _Authoring note: the game is set somewhere in Maine — the player does not know this yet._

**Who:** Zanzibar alone. His group split up after an argument to search for help in different directions. Details TBD.

**Condition:** Hungry. Canteen nearly empty.

**Mood:** Hardship without despair. Zanzibar is tired and preoccupied, but his optimism holds. The argument weighs on him; forward motion is how he copes.

### What the player should feel

- **Immediate grounding** — A person in a real place, not a title card or lore dump
- **Quiet tension** — Something went wrong before this moment; we don't know what yet
- **Sympathy for Zanzibar** — He keeps going anyway
- **Curiosity** — Where is he going? What is he looking for? What was the argument?

### What we hold back (for now)

- Identity and whereabouts of the group
- What the argument was about
- What "help" means in this context
- That the setting is Maine
- How the forest connects to the central facility

See [World & Style Guide](world-and-style.md) for environment and tone.

## Narrative Direction

The story should:

- **Anchor the player** — Give a clear reason to explore each technology
- **Create stakes** — Why does learning this matter? (Power a community? Prevent disaster? Unlock a mystery?)
- **Respect the science** — Characters and events should not contradict real physics/engineering
- **Allow discovery** — Learning feels like uncovering secrets, not being lectured

### Beat — Shelter and supplies

After arriving, Zanzibar explores the **main building**. He finds practical survival resources before he understands what the facility is for. This beat may take **more than one day** — wandering, resting, eating, sleeping again before moving on.

| Room / area                 | Story role                                                    | Notes                                  |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Two-story garage**        | First major interior space; scale of the campus               | _Upper level TBD — storage? workshop?_ |
| **Conference room**         | Hints that this was a working institution, not abandoned ruin | Clues TBD — whiteboards, agenda, logos |
| **Kitchen / casual dining** | Human warmth; Zanzibar can finally eat                        | Rations available                      |
| **Rations supply**          | Resolves immediate hunger from the forest                     | Found in or near kitchen               |

**Rest beat:** After a meal, the sun sets. Zanzibar finds a place to lie down. The facility is quiet. He has shelter and food — a small victory before the hard work begins. He may repeat this pattern (explore by day, rest by night) several times before the next major beat.

_Player goal: get oriented, eat, establish a base. No power yet._

### Beat — Learn, engage, celebrate

With shelter established, Zanzibar digs deeper. This arc may unfold over **multiple days**: reading one day, struggling with controls the next, succeeding and celebrating after that.

| Discovery                 | Story role                                                          | Learning / gameplay                                        |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Library (first visit)** | Study material to prepare for hydro                                 | Physical docs and/or limited access; sets up core concepts |
| **Hydro control room**    | First major system — remote control of the campus hydroelectric dam | Player figures out how to engage the dam                   |
| **Power online**          | First restoration win — lights, systems, charge port                | Simulation gate: dam engagement succeeds                   |
| **EV buggy**              | Campus mobility; reward for restoring power                         | In garage or nearby; charge and drive                      |
| **Buggy drive**           | Earned celebration — explore the campus                             | Charge the EV; drive around; enjoy what he has learned     |

**Emotional arc:** Curiosity → competence → joy. Zanzibar goes from hungry wanderer to someone who brought a dam back online and is cruising the campus in an electric buggy. This is the first time optimism pays off at scale.

_Player goal: learn enough to engage the hydro system, restore power, enjoy the campus. Allow room for false starts and overnight breaks._

### Beat — Holo-readers and the facility revealed

**After power is working**, Zanzibar returns to the library. With electricity restored, the **holo-readers** come alive — the way people study in the future. Immersive, interactive, layered with detail the paper shelves couldn't offer.

Through the holo-readers, Zanzibar learns what this place really is:

- A **secret research facility**, funded by the **Department of Energy** (or equivalent — details TBD)
- The campus hydro plant is **high-efficiency technology** — beyond what was typical of late-20th-century dam construction
- This is the first clear **sci-fi beat**: we assume energy technology continued to advance over the next ~100 years, and this facility was on the leading edge

_Specific names, mission statements, classification levels, and holo-reader UX — to be developed later._

| Revelation                          | Story role                                                      | Notes                                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| DoE-funded secret research facility | Answers "what is this place?"                                   | Unlocks deeper mystery — why secret? why abandoned?           |
| Advanced hydro                      | Explains why the dam behaves differently than textbook examples | Good place for near-future liberties; core physics still real |
| Holo-readers as study tool          | Model for how learning works going forward                      | Power-gated; rewards restoring electricity                    |

**Design note:** Real physics and engineering principles stay intact. Sci-fi enters through **advanced implementations** — higher efficiency, better materials, smarter controls — not through breaking how energy works.

### What Part I (early beats) teaches

The opening beats are the tutorial disguised as story:

1. **Explore** — The facility rewards looking everywhere
2. **Read** — The library holds answers the control room assumes you know
3. **Apply** — Engaging the dam requires understanding what you read
4. **Reward** — Power unlocks comfort (light, charge, mobility)
5. **Return and deepen** — Holo-readers reveal the bigger picture once power is on

The hydro simulation gate is the template for every chapter that follows. The holo-reader beat establishes that **restoration unlocks understanding**, not just convenience. Mid-week beats repeat and deepen this pattern; the elevator reveal pays off a full week of curiosity.

See [World & Style Guide](world-and-style.md) for hub layout and [Learning Objectives](../learning-objectives.md) for hydro assessment targets.

### Beat — The hidden elevator (Part I ending)

Near the **end of the week**, Zanzibar discovers a **hidden elevator** — concealed somewhere on the campus (location TBD). It goes **down**.

This is the Part I climax: not a simulation win, but a **threshold**. The surface facility has given up its secrets for now; something larger waits below.

| Beat                           | Story                                                        | Player experience                                              |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discovery                      | A week of exploration leads to something previously missed   | Satisfaction — the campus still had secrets                    |
| Reveal                         | An elevator, hidden in plain sight or behind a solved puzzle | Wonder — vertical scale; the facility is deeper than it looked |
| Descent (or the promise of it) | Zanzibar realizes where it leads — down                      | Cliffhanger — _The End… and the beginning of Part II_          |

_Whether the player rides the elevator in Part I's final scene or the game cuts on the doors opening — TBD._

## Tone & Voice

- **Mysterious and solitary** — Myst-inspired. The player explores alone, piecing together what happened.
- **Hopeful** — Rediscovery, not despair. Each restored facility is a step toward rebuilding.
- **Minimal narration** — The world teaches through environment, found documents, and machinery — not tutorials.
- **A defined protagonist** — Zanzibar Nuhero (Tintin-inspired): smart, optimistic, confident, resilient. Brings energy and optimism to a quiet world. See [Characters](characters.md).

See [World & Style Guide](world-and-style.md) for full setting, visual direction, and audio direction.

## Integration with Learning

Each story beat should:

1. Introduce a problem that requires understanding a concept
2. Provide resources (in-world docs, NPCs, environment) to learn
3. Require application in a simulation or puzzle
4. Reward with story advancement and new areas/tech
