# Narrative Design

[DRAFT] — Principles and practices for story integration in Atomic Adventures.

## Principles

1. **Show, Don't Tell** — Prefer in-world discovery over exposition dumps.
2. **Fail Forward** — Failed simulations or puzzles should teach, not punish.
3. **Character Through Tech** — NPCs and factions can embody different technologies or philosophies.
4. **Consistency** — World rules (physics, engineering) stay consistent.
