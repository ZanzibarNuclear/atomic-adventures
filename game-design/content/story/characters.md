## Protagonist

### Zanzibar Nuhero

**Role:** Player character. The player sees the world through Zanzibar's eyes and makes his choices.

Zanzibar is the heart of the story — smart, optimistic, and a positive influence wherever he goes. He is confident and self-driven, with a healthy spirit of adventure (Tintin is a touchstone). He is also human: he may hit moments of despair when a problem seems insurmountable, but he always pulls through with fresh ideas and renewed resolve.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Full name** | Zanzibar Nuhero |
| **Archetype** | Adventurer-engineer; curious investigator who learns by doing |
| **Voice** | Direct, warm, occasionally wry. Reacts to discoveries with genuine interest, not lecture-mode. |
| **Strengths** | Quick learner, calm under pressure, sees problems as puzzles worth solving |
| **Flaws** | Can push ahead before he fully understands; optimism sometimes outruns preparation |
| **Relationship to player** | The player *is* Zanzibar — no separate avatar or silent protagonist |

#### Personality (established)

- **Smart** — Grasps technical concepts when given the right clues; connects ideas across chapters
- **Optimistic** — Believes restoration is possible; his energy contrasts the dormant world
- **Positive in groups** — Lifts others (when NPCs appear); collaborative rather than domineering
- **Confident and self-driven** — Acts without waiting for permission; investigates, then decides
- **Resilient** — Despair is temporary; setbacks lead to new approaches, not quitting
- **Adventurous** — Drawn toward the unknown; treats each facility as a mystery worth unraveling

#### In-game presence

Zanzibar is rarely narrated in third person. The player experiences:

- **Passage text** — Second person ("You reach the control room") or Zanzibar's internal reaction to what he sees
- **Found documents** — Zanzibar's marginal notes optional; keep sparse
- **Simulation moments** — Zanzibar is the operator at the controls
- **Failure beats** — Brief frustration, then a nudge toward the manual or a new angle — never humiliation

He is thinking about an **argument with his group** — they split up to look for help in different directions. Who they are, what the fight was about, and what help they need: all **TBD**.

## Character–Story Integration

Each chapter should give Zanzibar a **personal stake** in that technology's restoration, not just a technical puzzle:

1. **Emotional hook** — Why does *this* facility matter to him right now?
2. **Curiosity hook** — What question does he need answered before he can leave?
3. **Competence arc** — He starts uncertain, grows through found knowledge, succeeds through understanding

See [Story Overview](story-overview.md) for chapter beats and [World & Style Guide](world-and-style.md) for tone constraints.
